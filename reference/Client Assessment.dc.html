Notes for this page
· Purpose: front‑end qualification for outbound outreach to Inovo's ICP (CIO / CTO / CISO / compliance leads at regulated organizations), deployable under Inovo's brand or a partner's.
· Tailoring: hero, problem, stakes, playbook title, form labels, question wording, and recommendations all switch with the vertical. Question wording uses vertical tokens (regulated data type, framework, who audits) over a shared NIST CSF 2.0 spine, so scoring stays comparable across verticals.
· Scoring: 24 questions × 0–3 points; six equally weighted functions; tiers Exposed < 40 · Developing < 60 · Established < 80 · Resilient ≥ 80. Weights/tiers are defined in the logic and can be made editable like the MSP page if wanted.
· Routing (co‑delivery): in partner mode the payload lists both the partner's inbox and sales@inovois.com; in Inovo mode, Inovo only. Actual send is stubbed.
· Placeholders: playbook PDFs per vertical; partner logo; Calendly URLs; vertical copy should be reviewed by Inovo's practice leads.
