How the score is computed
1. Each answer carries 0–3 points (worst → best). Each domain has 4 questions, so domain max = 12.
2. Domain % = points ÷ 12 × 100.
3. Score = Σ(domain % × weight) ÷ Σ weights, rounded to a whole number 0–100.
4. Level = first threshold the score meets, top‑down. Domain status: gap < {{ cfg.gapBelow }}%, watch < {{ cfg.watchBelow }}%, strong otherwise.
5. Recommendations: one per domain, ordered by (100 − domain %) × weight — biggest weighted exposure first.

Assumptions

· Audience is the MSP owner/operator; copy speaks to them as the hero, Inovo as the guide (StoryBrand).
· Six domains × 4 questions; every answer scores 0–3. Questions were written by me from the MSP‑page positioning; content review by Inovo's CISO team is expected.
· Lead gate sits after the score preview: score and level are free; per‑domain detail and recommendations require the form.
· Work‑email validation rejects common personal domains (gmail, yahoo, outlook, hotmail, icloud, aol, proton).
· Partner inquiries and assessment leads share one lead store but carry a type field so they route separately.
· Recommendations map to the service names on inovois.com (Security Maturity Level Assessment, vCISO, IR Planning, CIS Benchmark Hardening, SOC 2 / CMMC / ISO Readiness, Risk Assessment, MSP Cyber Consulting & Training).

Functional vs. stubbed

Functional: full 24‑question flow with validation, back navigation and resume; weighted scoring; level assignment; per‑domain breakdown; recommendation engine; lead + partner form validation; white‑label brand switching driven by a config object; admin lead list with structured payload; live weight/threshold/CTA editing; localStorage persistence of leads, config, and in‑progress answers; console analytics events.
Stubbed: HubSpot and Zapier calls (payload is built and logged, not sent); report email and PDF download; authentication on the admin view; server‑side storage.

Prototype → production

· Backend: a small API (e.g. serverless) to receive the payload, persist it, and call HubSpot/Zapier server‑side so tokens never reach the browser. Add rate limiting and honeypot/Turnstile on both forms.
· Multi‑tenant brand config: move the brand object to a database keyed by partner slug or hostname (assess.inovois.com vs. security.partnerdomain.com); admin UI to edit it; asset upload for logos.
· Content management: question bank, weights, thresholds, and recommendation copy in a versioned table so Inovo can tune scoring without a deploy; keep assessment.version on every submission.
· Report delivery: server‑rendered PDF with the partner brand; transactional email (Postmark/SendGrid); optional CISO booking embed.
· Analytics & attribution: UTM capture into the payload, funnel events to GA4/HubSpot, per‑partner attribution.
· Accessibility & QA: keyboard traversal of the radio groups, focus management between steps, WCAG contrast check on partner colors (warn admins when a chosen primary fails 4.5:1 on white).
· Legal: privacy notice + consent checkbox on both forms; partner agreement language on data ownership.
